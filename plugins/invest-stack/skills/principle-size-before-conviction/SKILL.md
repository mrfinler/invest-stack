# Size Before Conviction

- **Weight first.** Every buy or sell idea says what weight the position reaches, against its target in `settings/targets`, with the amount in USD and THB.
- **Use his real cash flow.** Size ideas in units of his average monthly deposit from the ledger. "Half of one month's DCA" is a size he can feel.
- **Concentration check.** Count look-through exposure. VOO already holds the largest US companies, so a direct NVDA position on top of VOO is a bigger NVDA bet than its own weight shows. Compute the overlap when it matters.
- **Small first.** A new idea starts at or below 5% of the portfolio unless Ped set a target above that.
- **Costs count.** Dime's fee per order, the FX spread, and any realized gain from a sale go into the comparison.
