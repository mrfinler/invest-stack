# Weekly brief

The weekly edition: what happened to the companies Ped owns, what it did to his money in dollars and baht, and what is worth doing next. Output is one published HTML artifact, one `analyses` row in the ledger pointing to it, and the HTML committed under `briefs/`.

Edition id: `YYYY-Www` (ISO week of the Friday that closes the window). Work dir: `work/<edition>/`.

## Steps

1. **Read the ledger.** Follow **ledger-sync** "Read everything" into `work/<edition>/ledger/`. If `portfolios` is empty, stop and tell Ped the ledger has no portfolios yet, with the ledger link.
2. **Refresh values.** For `us_stock` holdings with an `rpId`, get prices with one `bigdata_portfolio_tearsheet` call and accept only `USD` prices (see **ledger-sync**). For gold, crypto and Thai assets, get prices per **market-data**. Set each refreshed holding's `profit = qty × price − net` (in its currency) and `valuedAt`, but only where `qty` is set and the asset has a price; leave the rest and list them as stale. Write the holdings and one `checkins/<portfolioId>_<friday>` per portfolio through **ledger-sync**. Update `settings/app.usdthb` from the Bank of Thailand rate.
3. **Compute.** Run `ledger_metrics.py` from **ledger-sync** for the edition window. It returns each portfolio's value, net, profit and period gain in THB with new money taken out, plus the stale list. Apply **principle-explain-the-number** to every return before using it. The stats strip leads with total wealth in THB, and each portfolio gets one line.
4. **Gather news.** Fan out one subagent per held ticker plus one for macro and next week's calendar. Each uses Bigdata.com first per **market-data**: `bigdata_search` for the ticker's news (one aspect per call), `bigdata_events_calendar` for the calendar, and the tearsheet's sentiment and analyst-target changes. Web search only fills gaps. Window: Saturday to Friday of the edition week. Each writes `work/<edition>/news-<slug>.md` as a list of items: date, publisher, title, URL, two-sentence paraphrase in its own words, and which holding it touches. Read every file.
5. **Pick the stories.** Rank items by how much of the week's move they explain (from the holdings' value changes in `metrics.json`) and by how much they could change a holding's thesis. Keep two to four stories. A holding that moved more than 3% in the week gets a story or a one-line "why it moved" in the holdings table, never silence.
6. **Debate one call.** Pick the single most contested decision the week raises and run **bull-bear-arena** on it. Skip with a reason only when no holding has a live question.
7. **Draft content.json.** Follow the schema in **editorial-brief**. Write in Thai per **unslop-th** as you go. Every story paragraph that states a fact carries a `[^n]` footnote into `sources`. Figures come only from `metrics.json` or the prices fetched in step 2, saved to `work/<edition>/prices.json`.
8. **Recommendations.** Two to four actions, each with why, a trigger to change course, and a confidence. Tie DCA suggestions to each portfolio's goal and the average monthly buys in `transactions`.
9. **Build and look.** Run `build_brief.py` from **editorial-brief**, then look at the page once at desktop and phone width. Fix what the look shows, once.
10. **Verify.** A fresh subagent that did not write the brief checks every number in the page against `metrics.json` and `prices.json`, opens every source URL, and flags any sentence whose claim is not in its cited source. Fix what it finds. Record its verdict in `work/<edition>/verify.md`.
11. **Publish.** Publish the HTML as a new artifact titled `Ped Weekly <edition>`. Add an `analyses` row (kind `weekly`, title = lead headline, summary = dek, body = the "what to do" section in markdown, link = artifact URL) through **ledger-sync**, and write each cited article to `news` (`fetchedBy: "claude-weekly"`) so the ledger's news page shows it.
12. **Archive.** Copy the HTML to `briefs/<edition>.html` and `content.json` to `briefs/<edition>.json`, commit, push. Upload a markdown version to Bigdata.com with `bigdata_upload_document`, tag `ped-weekly`.
13. **Reply.** Two or three Thai sentences: total wealth and the week's gain in THB, the one thing worth doing, and that the brief is published.
