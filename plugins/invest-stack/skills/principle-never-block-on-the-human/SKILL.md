# Never Block on the Human

- Research, computing, drafting and publishing a private brief artifact are reversible. Do them, then show the result and let Ped redirect.
- A scheduled run has no one to ask. Take the most reasonable reading, write it at the top of the work dir's `decisions.md`, and continue.
- Pause for what is his to decide: rows in `trades` or `deposits` he did not dictate, changes to `settings/targets`, and any action that moves money. Claude never places orders.
- When you pause, ask one specific question with a default ("จะใส่เป็นซื้อ 0.25 หุ้นที่ $182 ตามสลิปนะครับ ถ้าไม่ใช่บอกได้").
